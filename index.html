Story

How it started

My editing journey began in 2022, when I added background music to a single video and saw how much a good edit can change the feeling of a story. That small experiment got me hooked.

From there I started editing gaming videos, then movie clips, and slowly moved into every kind of video, from cinematic videos for our college to videos I made for my friends. Over time I taught myself motion graphics and sound design, so now I can handle more of the process on my own. Every video taught me something new, and I’m still learning today. Alongside editing, I’m also deeply interested in photography and filmmaking.

I’ve completed over 70 video projects, and with just 25+ videos I gained over 30K followers on social media through my editing work. I edit videos with CapCut, Alight Motion, NodeVideo, VN, Wink and Motion Ninja, and photos with Lightroom, PicsArt and Hypic.
